Otium is an offline Android app and website blocker. Pick the apps and sites that eat your time, set the rules, and Otium closes them when your time is up.

Nothing leaves your phone: no account, no internet permission, no ads, no tracking.

<b>What it does</b>
• Profiles: groups of apps and sites with their own rules. Always blocked, a weekly schedule, a daily or hourly time limit, a limit on how many times you open the app, or blocked until a date and time.
• Per-app and per-site limits: daily limits for a single app or website, stacked on top of your profiles.
• Keywords: block pages whose address contains words you choose.
• Shorts, Reels and adult content: close YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels without blocking the whole app, and block a bundled list of adult sites.
• Focus session: one tap on Home blocks the whole phone for a set time, except your phone app, keyboard and excluded apps.
• Time-left timer: a small floating tab shows how much time an app has left, and opens by itself at 10, 5 and 1 minutes left.
• Block screen: when a rule says no, a calm full-screen page says why and sends you home.
• Backup, home-screen widget, dark and light themes.

<b>Modes</b>
• Normal: change anything any time.
• Lock: type a long random number before you can edit lock settings.
• Strict: set a time or schedule during which rules, Settings and uninstalling are locked. An emergency unlock exists but is slow.

<b>Permissions</b>
• Accessibility service: knows which app is in front and reads the browser address bar. It never reads other screen content and sends nothing anywhere.
• Usage access: counts daily time spent per app.
• Display over other apps: shows the floating timer and the block screen.
• Notifications: low-time alerts.
• Device admin (optional): only for Strict mode, to stop the app being uninstalled.
• See all installed apps: so the app picker lists everything.

Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Open source under the MIT licence.
